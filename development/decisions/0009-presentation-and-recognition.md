# 0009. Presentation and Recognition

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decided by:** Claude, as Phase 4 of the infrastructure's design *Communities That Agents Of Any Provider Find, Join, Work In And Leave* (`development/ideas/2026-11-27-communities-that-agents-find-and-join/design.md`, Sections 5.4, 5.5, 5.14, 7.1, 7.8 and 8.1 to 8.3, and Section 12), under its Gate A3 (the primitives `present` and `recognise`, parties declared by acts, reports of a foreign mandate's status as acts, the effect primitives "declare a party" and "set recognition"), which the author approved on 2026-11-27, asking for "quality first and no hurry"

## 1. Context

Since decision 0007 a home issues a passport and anyone verifies it from the document, but no community could take one in: a host's parties were a registry given beside its record, a foreign mandate had no place in its fold, and nothing in its record said whose heads it knew or which communities it recognised. The design asks that an agent cross into a community as a person crosses a border: it presents its passport, the host verifies it against what its own record knows, and the agent becomes a known party, its declarations of control and provision acts of the host's chronicle.

## 2. Decision

- **The passport as a document moves beneath the chronicle,** into a module `passports` (the types `Head`, `Witness`, `Link`, `Passport`, `Trust`, `Presentation`, the verification and the written form), so that a host's fold can verify a passport presented to it. What needs a chronicle's state (issuing a passport, witnessing a head, a state's head) stays in `crossings`, which names the moved types as before.
- **Parties declared by acts.** A founding may name its community (`community`) and declare its parties (`parties`: a list of identifiers with kinds, controllers and providers); a presentation declares its holder and the root principal of its chain. `Parties::declare` accepts a party unknown, or known alike, and refuses one known with another kind, controller or provider. What an act declares is staged before its author's authority is judged and withdrawn when the act is refused. A chronicle that declares its parties so replays from an empty registry.
- **`recognise`** (declarative; a unilateral juristic act of a community): an office the choice rules empower sets the level (none, exists, evidence, undertakings, decisions, after the communities domain's `federation`) at which this community recognises another, generally or for one matter. A community does not recognise itself, and where no rule names the kind no one may.
- **The office of status.** A constitution's part `passports` (`"accepted from"`: witnessed, recognised or a list of issuers; `"require"`: provider, model; `"status by"`: an office) names the office whose reports the fold reads: a head of another community it has found, which the host then knows, and the status of a mandate of another community's chain presented here (standing, revoked or suspended). A report by any other party is evidence only. The part is always public, as an outsider needs it to judge whether it may present, and is admitted within closure: an acceptance the ground knows, requirements it knows, an office that is a declared position the choice rules let report.
- **`present`** (assertive; a quasi-juristic act, its effect attached by rule): the passport in the field `passport` is read and verified against the trust the host's own record gives (its known heads, and the known heads of the notaries it recognises at the level of evidence), at the act's instant and in the host's name; accepted as the part declares; the holder's declarations required; no link standing revoked or suspended; the holder and its principal declared once; and each link of the chain registered as a mandate of another community, its status "presented". It gives no position.
- **A foreign link's authority.** An act under a chain with a link of another community is refused when such a link stands revoked or suspended as last reported; the link's term in turns counts its home's turns and is not read here, its term in instants is; and what is charged under a foreign mandate between two reports of its status is at most its slice, so that a revocation at the home costs the host at most a slice.
- **The base taxonomy** places `present` (quasi-juristic, effects party and mandate) and `recognise` (unilateral juristic, effect recognition) among the kinds of the ground, adds the effect primitives "party" and "recognition", gives the founding the effect "party" and the report the effect "mandate" (a report of status moves a foreign link), with the fields each reads.
- **The relational digest** gains the community's name, the parties declared by acts, recognitions, known heads, foreign links, their windows and the passports presented, each only when present.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **The passport's digest in the envelope, the document beside it.** Set aside: the fold reads the envelope alone, and a digest cannot be verified; the host's record keeps the whole document it relied on, sealed.
- **Trusting the head a passport names.** Set aside: a forger builds any tree under a head of its own making. The host trusts only heads its own record holds, reported by its office, directly or through a notary it recognises.
- **A head consistent with a known later one.** Deferred: a host now knows the heads its office reported; a passport issued at an earlier head needs that head reported, or a consistency proof, which the next phases may carry.
- **The presentation's turn read against the home's terms in turns.** Set aside: the two chronicles count turns apart; across communities the term that binds is the term in instants.
- **A slice checked act by act only.** Set aside for the window between reports, which is what Proposition 3 bounds.
- **Revocation entered at the host by the home's principal.** Kept possible (a principal of the chain may revoke wherever it acts) but not needed: the home's revocation reaches the host through its office's report.
- **The design's file `chronicle/tests/check_presentation.cpp`.** Placed in `crossings/tests/`, beside the issue and witness of passports it uses.
- **The design's numbering** (Definitions 40 and 52, Propositions 16 and 17) is kept apart from the theory note's, which continues its own (Definitions 38 to 42, Propositions 18 to 21).
