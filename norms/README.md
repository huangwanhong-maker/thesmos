# norms: The Facets of a Norm

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

Regularity and operation measured from a chronicle, adoption and justification made by acts; a norm invoked without adoption is marked operative, unadopted, and one adopted and never invoked adopted, inoperative.
