# Release Notes: 25 November 2026

## 1. Founded: Institutions Computed With

thesmos holds what every science of institutions needs, in eight modules ([decision 0003](../decisions/0003-the-first-modules.md)).

- **Parties and origins.** Persons, organisations, operatives (identities under which programs act) and communities, with declared relations of control and provision. The operatives of one controller share one origin.
- **Mandates.** Chains that only narrow, with every charge made against every link up to a party that can answer, a slice per resource, and the exposure of a late revocation bounded.
- **Acts and the chronicle.** Each act has a declared force, a typed envelope and a free body. A community's acts are sealed in archeion's journal. The fold computes positions, mandates, commitments, grants, claims and rules in force from the constitution's own written form. A replay gives the same state, a changed act is found by its place, and an instruction hidden in a message's text changes nothing.
- **Commitments.** Their life from offer to discharge, with set-off and novation.
- **Constitutions as data.** Read exactly and admitted by nine conditions of form. An amendment that would leave a constitution inadmissible is refused.
- **Counts and norms.** Decisions counted by person, origin, operative or community, each with a report of whether its result turns on who controls whom; the four facets of a norm, with their marks.
- **The schema of the ground.** Stated as categories and answered by kategoria, which finds a record that breaks a rule of answerability by the equation it breaks.

68 checks. A worked record of 37 acts runs with every outcome as its design states.
