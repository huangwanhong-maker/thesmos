# thesmos

*Institutions computed with.* thesmos (θεσμός, what is laid down: an institution) holds what every science of institutions needs: parties of declared kinds and their origins, mandates whose chains only narrow, acts of declared force, commitments and their life, constitutions written as data and admitted by form, decision rules with their unit of count, the chronicle that seals a community's acts and the fold that computes its relational state, the facets of a norm, a community's holdings with the norms of access to them, the passports a home issues for the agents it sends and their presentation at a host that recognises the home, and a taxonomy of behaviour in levels of description that keeps normative acts apart from conduct, records epistemic behaviour only by agreement, and lets communities extend the society's law by declared forms.

It is one of the foundation libraries of [Noetarium, the Science and Humanity Infrastructure](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Noetarium-The-Science-and-Humanity-Infrastructure), and a project in its own right. Status: `draft`, founded on 2026-11-25.

## 1. Concepts

- **The ground.** A community's acts each carry one of five forces (Searle's classes of illocution), a typed envelope that the fold reads, and a free body that persons and models read. Duties and powers follow from envelopes alone, so an instruction carried in a body changes nothing.
- **The chronicle.** The acts a community admitted, sealed in archeion's hash-chained journal. Positions, mandates, commitments, grants, claims and rules in force are computed from it. A refused act changes nothing; a replay gives the same state; an altered act is found.
- **Answerability.** An operative (an identity under which a program acts) acts only under a chain of mandates whose root principal is a person, an organisation or a community; each link narrows the one above; every charge is made against every link up to the root.
- **Constitutions as data.** A community's order in Ostrom's seven kinds of rules, with its bodies, scenes, conversions, route for claims, custody of means and rules of amendment, admitted by nine conditions of form. The fold reads the written form itself.
- **Counts by origin.** A decision declares its unit of count; by origin, the operatives of one controller count once, and a decision that turns on a declared relation of control is reported as turning.
- **Holdings and the norms of access.** A community holds resources of many kinds (data, documents, tools, budgets, capacity, knowledge, briefings), each with a custodian and a provenance, and declares who may consult, use, deposit, modify, lend or withdraw them, in the grammar of institutions. The most specific norm decides, a prohibition prevails at equal specificity, and what no norm permits is refused unless its kind is declared open. A holding is public, members' or an office's, and a norm says whether what is reached may be sent to a service outside the community. An agent that enters a community is briefed from its record: the mission, what it may do with each holding and by which norm, its duties and the open work; an outsider reads its public profile.
- **Passports.** An agent sent beyond its home carries a passport: the document its principal's home issues from its record, every link of the agent's chain of mandates with its proof of inclusion under the home's head, the notaries' witnesses of that head, and the agent's declarations. Anyone who knows the home's head, or a notary's that witnessed it, verifies the passport from the document alone, as it is shown at an instant, at a host and with a credential whose digest the mandate names. A mandate may name the communities in which it may be used, its holder's credential by digest, a term in instants and a reservation; a principal who sends one agent to several hosts issues a split of its mandate for each, each reserving its ceilings, so that the hosts together never admit more than the whole. The ground keeps no registry of identities: a person's home may be a household of that person and their agents.
- **Presentation and recognition.** An agent crosses into a community as a person crosses a border. It presents its passport by the act `present`, the host's fold verifies it against the heads the host's own record knows (reported by the office of status its constitution names, directly or through a notary it recognises), and the agent and its principal become known parties holding no position until the host's rules give them one. A community recognises another by the act `recognise`, at a level (that it exists, that its attestations count as evidence, that its envoys' undertakings bind it, that its decisions are honoured), generally or for one matter. Parties are declared by acts (a founding its founders, a presentation its agent and principal), so that a record replays from an empty registry. A foreign mandate presented here stands as its home was last reported, and what is charged under it between two reports is at most its slice, so that a revocation at the home costs a host at most a slice.
- **Levels of description and epistemic behaviour by agreement.** The taxonomy's second version, the base in force and a revision of the first along which records carry, describes one occurrence at five levels: what was intended (E, epistemic behaviour), what was done (O), what was recorded (M), what a community took it to be (S, indexed by that structure) and what an order qualifies it as (N, its constituted acts and qualified conduct), an occurrence at several levels at once. Facets cross the levels (stage, polarity, agency, modality, intention, scope), nine bridges join them, and practices such as a diplomatic protest are templates of linked descriptions that communities extend. Epistemic behaviour enters the record only under a disclosure agreement between an agent's principal and the community, ratified by each side under its own rule, its record a holding consulted for the purposes the terms admit, ended by denunciation after notice and then kept, returned or erased; outside an agreement only the fact that reasoning took place is kept. No norm attaches an effect to epistemic behaviour alone (G10).
- **Items, routes and rulings.** Every directive has a route inside its community. A request that names a type opens an item (an application, a question, a petition, or a type the community declares; `apply`, `question` and `petition` are kinds of the base taxonomy's third version that count as such requests), answered on its route by a body or an office within a term, never by a party to it, and overdue after its term. An application is decided by the boundary rule of the position: at once by rule, openly, by sponsorship or after a conversion; by an admission from the body that decides or the office that assigns; by a draw from a committed seed. A ruling enters the community's register and stands as precedent: a second question on its subject is refused with the ruling cited, and only the body that reviews rulings may overrule it. A petition is met by a deposit checked, adopted through a proposal, or carried. A community that declares its routes declares one for every directive (G8′); one that declares none reads them from what it declares.
- **Transmission, counts and compacts.** Where a community declares that operatives transmit to their provider, an agent's reading of a holding counts as sending it to its model's provider, and the norms of access must name that provider: a declared counts-as rule of the base taxonomy's fifth version, off in every existing record. Decisions may count by provider (a provider's agents carry one voice) or by declared weight, under thresholds at exact fractions or double majorities of members and weight. Communities ratify compacts by their own amendments, with terms whose digest they share, reservations closed or open article by article, and withdrawal after notice; a compact's standing is read across its parties' records, each party's ratification and withdrawal kept apart.
- **Posted work.** A community posts work whose life it declares as data: who posts a kind, who may take it and how (by a commitment, or by an award), how long a taking runs, its deliverable and check, its review (by another origin, by another provider, or by a position) with its verdicts, who accepts it, what follows a return, and what must come first. The life of an item is read from its record and the turn (open, waiting, taken, lapsed, delivered, reviewed, returned, accepted, withdrawn, overdue), every transition an act and the lapse and reopening effects by law; no item is held by more takers than its kind admits, and where the kind declares review by another provider every accepted item was reviewed so. A refusal of the life is a rule of the community, changed by amendment; an item lives under the life it was posted under.
- **Orders, terms and seats.** A community may declare its order, a label (a polity, an organisation, a treaty organisation, a league) that its profile shows beside its name and registries read, and that no fold reads; and in its part `external` the registries it says it is listed in and its envoys. A position may be held for a term, read as the trigger rule that ends a position held too long; a body may have a number of seats, and the fold refuses every act that would give it more members (decision 0014).
- **Behaviour.** What a society records falls into five categories: normative acts, finite in kind and constituted by their chronicle; conduct, open and continuous, evidenced by a behaviour journal joined to the chronicle act to episode; events; states; observations. A community declares kinds that count as kinds in force (their effects are their primitives', so declarations add none), categories of conduct as regions of a space of features, counts-as rules from conduct to a status a norm reads, trigger rules producing effects the ground marks as by law, and standards of recognition. The base taxonomy is data, admitted and revised on kategoria's ontologies. No institutional effect arises except by an admitted act or a declared rule over the record and its turns (G9).

The theory note is [docs/theory.md](docs/theory.md).

## 3. Building and Checking

Inside the infrastructure, from its root: `node development/tools/run-cpp.mjs foundations/thesmos thesmos`. thesmos uses the sibling libraries archeion, symploke, morphe, apodeixis, calx and kategoria, whose `ontologies` the taxonomy of behaviour stands on (decisions [0002](development/decisions/0002-scope-boundaries-and-dependencies.md) and [0008](development/decisions/0008-a-taxonomy-of-behaviour.md)).
