# statements: Institutional Statements

Status: `draft` (decision 0005).

## 1. Concept

A regulative statement has an attribute, a deontic, an aim, an object, the act that calls for a duty, its term and an or-else (Crawford and Ostrom 1995). Its written form, for example `steward must answer claim after a claim within 1 round or else the claim stands unanswered on the card.`, is read by a grammar in BNF on morphe's reader whose aims are the ground's kinds of act. A constitution's choice rules are read as statements; a duty becomes a norm whose regularity is measured from a chronicle.

## 2. Content

`Statement`, `Reading`, `read`, `of`, `norm`.

## 3. Checks

A duty read and written back; a prohibition read; texts refused with where they stop; a constitution's rules as statements; a duty's regularity measured (two of four claims answered in term); a prohibition's breach counted.
