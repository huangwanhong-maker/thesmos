# 0006. Holdings and the Norms of Access

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decided by:** Claude, at the author's request: "A community should can hold various resources, and should have some norm that let us know how to access such resources"; and, from the same discussion, that an agent that enters a community learns from the community what to do and the task's context, the context living in the community's record and never in an agent, a leader being an office that reads the same record. Visibility, transmission and the profile were added the same day from the first step of the author's design *Communities That Agents Find, Join and Leave* (the infrastructure's idea of 2026-11-27)

## 1. Context

thesmos held grants (a holder's use of a resource up to a ceiling), allowances and slices in mandates charged along the chain, acts of declared force, constitutions in Ostrom's seven kinds of rules, the four facets of a norm and rules in words. A community could keep no resource of its own beyond a grant, and nothing said who may consult, use, deposit, modify, lend or withdraw what it keeps, nor what an agent entering it should do. archeion has access rules in which the most specific decides, retention and provenance; the communities domain has gates of access for services.

## 2. Decision

- **A module `holdings`**, between `procedures` and `constitutions`, on the kernel, archeion's fixity and symploke's JSON:
  - a holding has an identifier, a kind (an open vocabulary), a custodian (an office of the constitution or a named party), a provenance (a digest by archeion's rule, a source, a licence), a container if it is kept within another holding, and, when metered, a unit and a quantity; a holding may declare review, so that what is deposited within it stands checked only when a party of another origin attests it;
  - norms of access in the grammar of institutions: an attribute (anyone; a kind of party; anyone who holds a position; a party whose origin holds a position; a position; the role custodian or depositor), a deontic (may, must, must not), an aim (consult, use, deposit, modify, lend, withdraw), an object (any holding, a kind, a holding with what is kept within it), conditions (under a mandate of given kinds and matters, within a quota a round for the party that answers, by need under a declared standard attested in the record by another origin, for a declared purpose) and an or-else;
  - the resolution declared and decided: among the norms that match, the most specific decide, by the object first (a holding, the nearer first, before a kind before any holding) and then the attribute (a role; a position the author holds itself; one held by the party that answers for it; an origin; anyone who holds a position; a kind of party; anyone); at that level a prohibition whose conditions hold prevails, else a permission whose conditions hold permits, else the request is refused with the conditions failed; where no norm speaks the request is refused unless the constitution declares the kind open to the aim. The verdict depends on the set of norms alone (Proposition 8);
  - **visibility**: a holding is public, members' (the default) or shown to named offices; visibility says who may know of it and read its description, while reading its content is an act under the norms;
  - **transmission**: a norm may say whether what is reached may be sent to a service outside the community (another provider of models, for instance), named in the act's field `transmit`; a permission silent on transmission permits none, and a prohibition silent on it forbids whatever is sent;
  - the briefing's entries: for a party, every holding it may know of and every aim on which a norm speaks or open access applies, by one sweep over the norms, equal to decide over every such holding and aim (Proposition 10); the duties of access; norms written in words; the written form `holdings` and `access` of `noetarium.constitution/1` read exactly.
- **The ground gains five kinds of act**, each declarative, made by virtue of a norm of access: consult, deposit, modify, lend and withdraw; "use" serves both grants (with an `id`) and holdings (with a `holding`).
- **The fold** holds the constitution's holdings from the founding (a party custodian must be one of the community's parties), and admits an act of access only when the holding allows it and the norms permit it; a refusal names the norm that forbids it, the conditions failed, or the absence of any norm. A draw from a metered holding is taken from what is available (its quantity less what is lent until a term not yet passed) and, under a mandate, charged along the whole chain in that holding's unit (G4; Proposition 9). Every access admitted is recorded: who, who answers, which holding, what, how much, for what purpose, by which norm. An attestation naming a pending holding, by a party of another origin than the depositor's, checks it. Holdings and accesses enter the relational digest.
- **The briefing** (`chronicle/briefing.hpp`): the mission, the positions, the kinds of act open to the party, what it may do with each holding it may know of and by which norm (marked when its mandate does not cover the act), its duties, and the open work the record shows (deposits to check, offers to take, commitments owed, deliveries to accept, claims to answer, proposals to vote on). A leader is an office and is briefed from the same record.
- **The profile** (`chronicle/profile.hpp`): a constitution's policy of records may declare the visibility of each of its parts, of who holds which position and of the work posted (`"records": {"visibility": {part: ...}}`), public unless declared; the purpose, the boundary rules and the route for claims are always public. The profile for a reader gives the mission, the admission policy, the route for claims and each part shown to that reader (offices and holders, posted work, holdings, norms); the public profile, with no reader, is what a directory lists. The ground fixes no policy of what is public.
- **Admission and the nine conditions.** No tenth condition is added. The form of holdings and norms falls within closure (condition 1: every norm names a declared holding or kind, position, kind of party or role, and has an identifier of its own, one deontic and an aim; what is shown to an office names a declared position; the policy of records names only parts there are) and within the custody of means (condition 6: every holding is kept by a declared office or a named party, has a kind, a quantity only when metered and never below nothing, declared and acyclic containers, a known review and a digest by archeion's rule where one is given). Visibility is part of the policy of records (condition 9), which may keep no community's purpose or boundary rules from the public; a route for claims kept from the public falls under condition 4 (G8). A constitution with no holdings is admitted as before.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **archeion's access rules, used as they are.** Set aside: their effects are open, closed and restricted to roles for reading a unit; a community's norms need deontics, aims beyond reading, conditions that read the chronicle (quotas, need, mandates) and charges along chains, which are thesmos's.
- **The norms as choice rules of the constitution.** Set aside: choice rules say which kinds of act a position may make, with no object and no condition; access is about a holding.
- **A tenth condition of admission.** Set aside: the form required is closure and custody, both already conditions.
- **The most specific by attribute first.** Set aside for the object first, after archeion's convention that a rule on the unit itself decides before one on a class.
- **Norms read in words by the grammar of `statements`.** Deferred: the regulative core is written as JSON; reading norms of access in words is the next step of `statements`.
- **A loan returned by an act.** Deferred: a loan's amount returns to what is available at its term.
- **A permission silent on transmission permitting any.** Set aside: what leaves for a third party should leave only where a norm says it may; the incident that prompted the author's design was an agent stopped by its own check before sending a community's material to another provider.
- **Every part of a constitution members' unless declared public.** Set aside: rules are made to be known, and an outsider must judge whether to join; holdings, which are resources, are members' unless declared public.

## 4. Consequences

- The illustrative constitution `examples/workshop.json` declares six holdings and eleven norms of access.
- The communities domain's presets declare holdings, visibility and norms (with transmission) that fit their orders; the API answers `communities.holdings`, `communities.access`, `communities.briefing` and `communities.profile`, and `communities.run` gives briefings on the state it folds; Koinonia shows a community's holdings and norms, briefs its agents, and lists the communities' public profiles in a directory.
- Checks: `holdings` 22 (Proposition 8 on 3,000 random registers, transmission among the conditions; Proposition 10 on 1,000), `access` in the chronicle 23 (Proposition 9 on 40 random chronicles of 300 acts, Proposition 10 on the workshop, transmission, the profile), two in `constitutions`; 133 in all.
- Next: norms of access read in words; amendments that add holdings and norms; returns of loans by act; retention of holdings by archeion's schedules; joining by outside agents, and a directory that is itself a community (the next steps of the author's design).
