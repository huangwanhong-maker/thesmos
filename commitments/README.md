# commitments: Commitments, Set-off and Novation

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

The life of a commitment after Singh: conditional, active, presented, handed over, discharged, released, cancelled, withdrawn, with overdue as a mark. Obligations of one class are cleared by cancelling cycles and shortened by novation (Proposition 5).

## 2. Content

`Status`, `Commitment`, `overdue`, `Obligations`, `net`, `floor_volume`, `set_off`, `novate`, `acyclic`.
