# 0007. Passports

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decided by:** Claude, at the author's request ("I love the 'passport' like mechanism, it is very insightful for us investigate diplomacy, multilateral communications, etc. Though we may not need immediately, please also implement it"; "I agree households!"), as Phase 1 of the infrastructure's design *Communities That Agents Of Any Provider Find, Join, Work In And Leave* (`development/ideas/2026-11-27-communities-that-agents-find-and-join/design.md`, Sections 5.3 to 5.5, 7.1 and 12), which the author approved with its Gate A on the same day

## 1. Context

An agent sent to a community by a person or an organisation had no document of its own. Its authority lived in a mandate entered in a community's chronicle, and a community it went to had nothing to check but a credential that community had issued itself, so the host decided who answered for the agent. The design's answer is a passport: issued by the principal's home community (for a person who belongs to no other, a household of one person and their agents), carried by the agent, and verified anywhere from the document alone. The ground keeps no registry of identities. thesmos already held mandates whose chains only narrow, envelopes charged along chains, chronicles sealed in archeion's journal, Merkle trees with proofs of inclusion, and notaries that witness heads.

## 2. Decision

- **Mandates gain four parts**, each written into the envelope only when set, so that every mandate issued before keeps its canonical text and every seal stands: `communities` (where the mandate may be used beyond its home; narrowing reads none named as unrestricted, presentation as nowhere outside the home), `credential` (the SHA-256 digest of the holder's bearer credential; the fold refuses one not so written), `reserves` (its ceilings charged against the links above it when it is issued), and a term in instants (`since`, `until`, ISO 8601 in UTC as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ) beside the term in turns. A child narrows its parent in each: it names some of the communities its parent names, keeps or tightens each bound in instants. The fold refuses an act whose instant lies outside its mandate's term in instants.
- **Splits.** A link of a chain is either a delegation by its parent's holder, which the parent must allow (Definition 4 as it was), or a *split*: a part of the parent issued by the parent's own principal for the parent's own holder, which must reserve. This is what the design's Definition 39 (one mandate a host) needs: the person issues, under the operative's root, one child for each host, and the children's ceilings together never pass the root's.
- **Reservation.** When a reserving mandate is issued, each of its ceilings is charged against the links a charge under its parent reaches; a charge later made under it, or beneath it, is charged from the deepest reserving link down, since that link's ancestors hold its reservation already. A record with no reserving mandate is charged along whole chains, as before. A reservation stays charged when the mandate is revoked, because a host may still admit a slice before it learns of the revocation (Proposition 3).
- **Passports in `crossings`.** `issue` assembles, from a home's state, the passport of a mandate that stands there: every link of its chain as the act that issued it (its body left out), with its seal and its proof of inclusion under the home's head; the principal and the holder with their declared kinds, and for an operative its controller (which must have issued a link of the chain), provider and model. `witness` lets a notary attest a head in its own chronicle, the witness carrying the attestation's proof under the notary's head; a verifier rebuilds the attestation from the head it is shown. `verify` decides, from the document and the heads the verifier knows (a home's, or a notary's that witnessed the home's), whether every link is an act of the home, the chain stands, the holder and principal are those named, and, as the passport is shown, whether its term in instants holds, it names the host, and the credential's digest is the mandate's. The written form `noetarium.passport/1` is written and read by symploke, every part of unexpected shape named.
- **A holder of any kind.** The document names its `holder` rather than its operative (the design's word), so that a person's letter of credence, which the design also carries as a passport (its Definition 53), takes the same form; for an operative the declarations are required.
- **Pointers outside the seals.** Everything under the passport's `mandate` and `act` is what the act's envelope seals, so a verifier rebuilds the envelope from it; the node that serves the home and the address where the mandate's status is kept are written under `issuer`, outside every seal.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **A passport carrying only its holder's mandate.** Set aside: a host charges against every link up to the root as it knows the chain (the design's Proposition 17), and checks that the root answers; a passport carries the whole chain, each link under the one head.
- **Children for each host issued by the holder, as a delegation.** Set aside: the agent would issue its own visas, under a root that must allow delegation and the kind "mandate". The design's principal issues them; a split says so, and is admitted only when it reserves.
- **Splits that need not reserve.** Set aside: the parts of a whole the principal divides should never pass the whole; a part that does not reserve is no split, and a delegation by the holder that does not reserve stays possible, with Proposition 2's counterexample (two hosts admitting 160 under a root of 100) as its known cost.
- **A reservation released on revocation.** Deferred: releasing what a revoked part did not spend needs the host's final report of its use, a status report the next phase brings.
- **Witnesses as signatures.** Deferred, with signatures, which thesmos has planned: a witness is an act in the notary's own chronicle, proved by inclusion, as receipts are.
- **The words `operative` and `status at` as the design placed them.** Changed, as said above; every name in the design is provisional.

## 4. Consequences

- Checks: `crossings/tests/check_passports.cpp`, 25: a passport issued and verified, written and read back; a head no notary witnessed, a witness by a notary the verifier does not know or altered, a forged proof, an expired term, a credential whose digest differs and a host not named, each refused with its reason; what the home will not issue and what its fold refuses; on 300 random homes every passport verifies and every alteration of a sealed part is refused (Proposition 11); on 200 random homes reserving splits never pass the root and hosts that each charge their own never pass it together (Proposition 12), with the counterexample without reserving; a record made before this decision sealing as Python's hashlib computes it (Proposition 13). 158 checks in all.
- The theory note gains §10, Passports (Definitions 17 to 21, Propositions 11 to 13), and amends Definition 4 for splits.
- The infrastructure's communities domain gains the preset `household`; its API issues, witnesses and verifies passports (`communities.passport.issue`, `.witness`, `.verify`; `science.communities.issue_passport`, `witness`, `verify_passport`).
- Next (the design's Phase 4, behind its Gate A3): the act `present`, by which a host verifies a passport in its own fold and the operative becomes a known party; reports of a foreign mandate's status as acts; parties declared by acts.
