# kernel: Parties and Origins

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

A party is an identified participant of one declared kind: a person, an organisation, an operative or a community. Control and provision are declared relations; the origin of a party under control is the first party reached along control that is not an operative, and under provision its provider. A party is declared once (decision 0009): `declare` adds a party unknown, accepts one known alike, and refuses one known with another kind, controller or provider, so that a chronicle may declare its parties by acts.

## 2. Content

`Kind`, `Party`, `Parties` (with `declare` and `erase`), `Criterion`, `origin`, `answerable`.

## 3. Checks

In `mandates/tests`: origins along control, an undeclared controller, provision.
