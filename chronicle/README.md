# chronicle: The Chronicle and Its Fold

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

A community's admitted acts sealed in archeion's journal, and the fold that gives its relational state on the constitution's own rules, bodies, units of count and tempi (Proposition 1). The fold holds the constitution's holdings from the founding and admits an act of access (consult, use, deposit, modify, lend, withdraw) only when the holding allows it and the norms of access permit it, a draw from a metered holding charged along the mandate's chain (Proposition 9), every access recorded with its norm (decision 0006). The briefing (`briefing.hpp`) tells a party what the record asks of it: the mission, what it may do with each holding and by which norm, its duties and the open work (Proposition 10), over the holdings its visibility lets it know of. The profile (`profile.hpp`) gives what a reader may know of the community without joining: its mission, admission policy and route for claims always, and each part its policy of records shows to the reader; the public profile is what a directory lists. The schema of the ground, `schema/ground.cat`, states answerability as equations that kategoria answers. For passports (decision 0007) the fold refuses a mandate that names its holder's credential otherwise than by digest and an act outside its mandate's term in instants, charges a reserving mandate's ceilings against the links above it when it is issued, and charges what is drawn beneath a reserving link from that link down (Proposition 12). For the taxonomy of behaviour (decision 0008) the fold reduces an act of a declared kind to the kind of the ground it counts as, keeping the act as written and its reduct as its reading, checks the declared kind's conditions, lets a counts-as rule make an act of access a transmission, settles declared trigger rules to each act's turn and keeps the settlement only with an admitted act, admits a revision of the part `behaviour` by amendment, and reads an act's typed field `episode`; the behaviour journal and its join to the chronicle are in `conduct.hpp` (Propositions 15 and 16, invariant G9). For presentation and recognition (decision 0009) the fold declares the parties a founding names and those a presentation carries, staged before the author's authority is judged and withdrawn when the act is refused, so that a chronicle replays from an empty registry (Proposition 21); keeps the recognitions `recognise` sets, generally or for a matter; reads the reports of the office of status (another community's head, a foreign mandate's status); verifies a passport presented by `present` against the heads its own record knows and registers its links as mandates of another community (Proposition 18); and refuses an act under a foreign link revoked or suspended, and a charge that would pass a foreign mandate's slice before its status is next reported (Proposition 19). For disclosure agreements (decision 0010) the fold admits `agree` for the principal by an act it answers for (the community's choice rules not read for it) and for the community by an office its choice rules empower to agree, holds the agreement in force once both have ratified the same terms, holds its record as a holding of kind `epistemic record` with the norms of access its terms make, admits `denounce` as the terms allow, refuses a consult of the record once its retention has run, when it was erased, or by any but the principal when returned, and admits an act citing the record only for an admitted purpose after a consult for it (Proposition 25). In `conduct.hpp` epistemic episodes are recorded only under an agreement that covers them (`disclose`, `disclosed_by_agreement`, Proposition 24), and a chronicle and its journal are described at levels (`describe`). For items, routes and rulings (decision 0011) a request that names a type opens an item on its route, an application is decided by the position's boundary rule (at once, by an admission that names it, by an office, by a draw from a committed seed), a question is ruled, referred, answered or declined and a ruling enters the register (a second question on its subject refused with the ruling cited, an overruling only from the review body on a review), a petition is met by the check of its deposit, adopted with the proposal that carries it, or carried, every answer is recused from the item's parties, an office ends an assignment by `assign`, and `leave` with `all` leaves every position; the briefing gives a party's items, what it may answer and the rulings in force, the profile the register and the open and overdue items by type (Propositions 27 to 29). For posted work (decision 0012) a request naming a kind posts a work item, a commitment or an awarded offer takes it while it is open, a delivery is admitted while the taking is taken or returned within its redelivery, the kind's office enters its check, a review is admitted once on each delivery from the party the kind admits and never from the deliverer's origin, an acceptance from the kind's office or decision after a verdict it accepts, a poster withdraws an item no one holds; the kind, not the choice rules, says who makes each act; a deposit within a holding reviewed by another provider is checked only by another provider, its verdicts checking or recording; the briefing shows what a party may take, deliver, check, review and accept, the profile the posted work with its states (Propositions 30 and 31). Since decision 0013, where the constitution declares that operatives transmit to their provider, an operative's reading reaches its provider (`reach_of`), which the norms of access decide with the service the act declares, and each access records it; a decision is taken by its unit, rule and weights (`procedures::decide`); amendments ratify compacts with their terms and reservations, withdraw reservations and withdraw the community after the compact's notice (`change.compact`, `change.terms`, `change.reservations`, `change.withdraw reservation`, `change.withdraw compact`); the briefing gives the provider a party's readings reach, the counts-as rules in force and the compacts that stand (Propositions 36 and 38). Since decision 0014 a position's term is settled as its trigger rule, the fold refuses every act that would give a body more members than its seats (an application by rule to a full body closing declined), amendments change terms and seats (`change.term`, `change.seats`), the profile shows the order to every reader and the registries and envoys where the part `external` is shown, the briefing gives the order and lists terms among the rules by law, and a step with trigger rules keeps aside and restores only the parts settlement changes, never copying the state (Propositions 39 to 42).
