# constitutions: Constitutions as Data

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

The written form `noetarium.constitution/1` read exactly, Ostrom's seven kinds of rules with bodies, scenes, conversions, claims, custody, preserved properties, amendment and records; admission by nine conditions of form; the report of recognised against executable powers. A constitution may declare its holdings and the norms of access to them (`holdings`, `access`), read by the module `holdings` and admitted within closure (condition 1) and the custody of means (condition 6). Its policy of records may declare the visibility of each part (`records.visibility`), public unless declared; the purpose, the boundary rules and the route for claims stay public. It may declare its part `behaviour` (decision 0008), read by the module `behaviour`: kinds declared by counts-as, features and categories of conduct, counts-as and trigger rules, standards of recognition, extending the base taxonomy; their form is admitted within closure (condition 1), the scenes and positions they name declared. The taxonomy in force is kept beside the part as written. It may declare its part `passports` (decision 0009): from whom it accepts passports (`"accepted from"`: witnessed, recognised, or a list of issuers), what a presented agent must declare (`"require"`: provider, model), and the office of status whose reports the fold reads (`"status by"`); the part is always public, its office a declared position that the choice rules let report. It may declare its part `routes` (decision 0011): a route for applications, for questions (body, term, review body, subjects), for petitions by kind (met by a deposit within a holding that declares review, by a decision or amendment, or carried by an office), and for each type of item a declared kind opens, each possibly empty (`"none"`); the part is always public, and when declared condition 4 holds it to G8′. Entry may be `on application` (with the conditions `holders fewer than n` and `passport from c`, and since decision 0012 `work open of kind k` and `work open for the applicant's provider`) or `assigned by` an office, and exit `by the office`. It may declare its part `work` (decision 0012), the kinds of work it posts, read by the module `work` and admitted within closure (condition 1). Since decision 0013 a decision may count by provider or by weight, with its weights (`"weights"`, whole numbers or fractions `p/q`), and its rule may be a threshold at a fraction or a double majority written as an object and read into its canonical text; condition 2 names a rule the kernel does not know, a count by weight with no positive weight and a double majority not counted by weight. The part `external` lists the compacts ratified at the founding, by name or as records (`id`, `reservations`, `since`, `terms`), each held within closure (condition 1); `ratification` and `compact_ids` read which stand at a turn. Since decision 0014 a constitution may declare its order (`"order"`), a label no admission condition and no step of the fold reads, and in its part `external` the registries it says it is listed in (`"listed in"`) and its envoys as objects (`{"to", "holder", "position"}`) or positions; a boundary rule may declare a term in turns (`"term"`), read as the trigger rule that ends a position held for more than its turns (`term_triggers`, `triggers`), and a body its seats (`"seats"`), the most members it may have (`members`, `beyond_seats`); a term or seats that is no whole number of at least one is a fault of conditions 1 and 2.

## 2. Content

`Constitution`, `read`, `admission`, `Fault`, `recognised`, `mentions`, `permits`, `forbids`, `may_hold`, `wait`, `powers`; since decision 0014 `Envoy`, `term_triggers`, `triggers`, `members`, `beyond_seats`.

## 3. Checks

An illustrative constitution admitted; a breach of each condition found; a reserve declared; text that is not JSON refused; a norm of access naming an undeclared holding and a holding kept by no office found under conditions 1 and 6; the visibility of the workshop's parts, a route for claims kept from the public and a part shown to an undeclared office refused. The part `behaviour` and its faults are checked in `behaviour/tests/check_behaviour.cpp`, the part `passports` and its faults in `crossings/tests/check_presentation.cpp`, the part `routes` and its faults in `chronicle/tests/check_items.cpp`, and the order, the external data, terms and seats with their faults in `chronicle/tests/check_terms_and_seats.cpp`.
