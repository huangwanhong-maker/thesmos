# 0010. Levels of Description and Epistemic Behaviour by Agreement

- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decided by:** Claude, at the author's refinement of the taxonomy of behaviour (`development/ideas/2026-11-27-communities-that-agents-find-and-join/taxonomy-of-behaviour-v2.md`, its Sections 1 and 7a), built as its own phase before the design's Phase 5 at the author's word; the author asked for "a more fine-grain" taxonomy in which "an action can belong to multi-level", and that "the archive should also even record agent's thinking, the epistemic behavior … only record, when a community/agent on an agent have 'agreement'. But this feature should implemented"

## 1. Context

Decision 0008 classed behaviour in five categories: normative act, conduct, event, state, observation. The author refined it: the highest divisions are levels of description of one occurrence (epistemic, ontological, manifest, social and normative), an occurrence may be described at several at once, facets and bridges cross them, and practices such as diplomacy are patterns across them. Epistemic behaviour, which the first version ignored, enters a record only by disclosure under an agreement, and no norm attaches an effect to it alone.

## 2. Decision

- **The second version as a revision.** The base taxonomy's second version (`schema/behaviour-2.json`, `behaviour::base()`) revises the first (`schema/behaviour.json`, `behaviour::first_base()`, kept as adopted) by the machinery of decision 0008. Its map sends the normative act to the constituted act (the first form of level N), conduct to ontological conduct (O) and the observation to manifest conduct (M), and fixes events, states, every class and every kind of the ground. The revision is admitted directly (`revision_faults`) and by kategoria (`admit_revision`, the roots it recasts excepted from the fixed kinds), and records carry along it as the left Kan extension. The base in force is the second version; a part that extends "base 1" is read under it, since the revision renames nothing a part may name.
- **Levels, facets, bridges, practices** (`Level`, `Facet`, `Bridge`, `Practice` in `behaviour.hpp`; written only when declared, so a first version is written as before). There are five levels, E, O, M, S and N, each in a register. S and N are indexed by the structure or order that makes a description at them, and N comes in two forms, constituted acts and qualified conduct. The six facets are stage, polarity, agency, modality, intention and scope; stage and intention are ordered, and the formation of intent is epistemic alone. The nine bridges are declared with the pairs of levels each joins, and three practices are declared as templates of linked slots. A community declares practices of its own, never levels, facets or bridges.
- **Descriptions of occurrences** (`behaviour/levels.hpp`): a description of an occurrence at one level, of one kind there, with its structure, grade, label and facets; links by bridges; faults named; the record of descriptions as a record of kategoria's ontologies (`record_of`, held by `fits`); instances of a practice (`instances`); the levels at which an occurrence is described (`levels_of`). `chronicle/conduct.hpp` describes a chronicle and its journal at levels (`describe`): each act at N; each episode at O with its trace at M and its act by codified counts-as; each disclosure at E with its record at M, joined to the episode it preceded by enactment.
- **Invariant G10 (freedom of thought).** No counts-as rule through a category, and no declared condition, may read epistemic behaviour alone (`epistemic_only`, `level_read`; a feature declares its level, E or conduct's). Both admission and amendment refuse such a rule.
- **Disclosure agreements** (a new module `disclosure`: the terms `noetarium.disclosure/1`, their faults, their digest, coverage, and the norms of access they make). Two kinds of the ground are added: `agree` (commissive, a bilateral juristic act) and `denounce` (declarative), with the effect primitive agreement and its end by law. The principal ratifies by an act it answers for, never subject to the community's choice rules. The community ratifies by an office its choice rules empower to agree. Both must ratify the same terms; when they have, the agreement is in force and its record is held as a holding of kind `epistemic record`, with norms of access for the principal and each position of the body named. Each who may consult has a permission for the purposes admitted and a prohibition for those never admitted.
- **Ending an agreement.** A denunciation takes effect after the notice in the terms. The agreement also ends at its last turn. What was recorded is then kept until its retention runs out, returned to the principal alone, or erased.
- **Holdings' norms gain the attribute `party`,** which binds one party and those that act for it, the most specific attribute, so that an agreement's norms name its principal.
- **The record of epistemic behaviour.** `disclose` writes an entry `epistemic` (the agreement, the digest and size of what was disclosed, never the content) only under an agreement in force that covers the episode with room under its ceiling. Otherwise it writes an entry `reasoning`, holding only the party and the turn. `disclosed_by_agreement` verifies the journal. `joined` (Proposition 15) passes over epistemic entries.
- **Use as evidence.** An act that cites an epistemic record (its field `epistemic record`) does so for a purpose its agreement admits and never forbids, after a consult of the record for that purpose.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **Five categories kept, with E and S added as categories.** Set aside: one occurrence would belong to one category, while the author's point is that it belongs to several levels at once. Levels with descriptions and bridges give that membership directly.
- **Social and normative conduct under one kind of attributed conduct.** Set aside: the author's levels are kept as named. The bridges with two pairs become two relations in kategoria's terms.
- **The content of a disclosure in the journal.** Set aside: an erasure would break the journal's seals. The journal keeps the digest and the agreement's record keeps the content under its norms.
- **Agreements kept beside the constitution as compacts (`external.compacts`).** Set aside for now: an agreement has two sides, one of which is not a community, with terms of its own and an end. It is kept in the chronicle's state, its ratifications and denunciation being acts.
- **The community's side ratified by amendment.** Deferred to Phase 7, which ratifies and withdraws compacts by amendment. Here an empowered office ratifies, as `recognise` does (decision 0009).
- **A level fixed by the code alone.** Set aside: the levels are data of the base, admitted with five letters required, since the fold's descriptions name them.

## 4. Consequences

- **Checks: 28 new, 239 in all.** Every earlier record replays with its seals; the earlier checks pass with the second version in force.
  - `behaviour/tests/check_levels.cpp`, 12. The journal is held to an independent reckoning on 100 random runs (Proposition 24), and the fold's consults to an independent reading of the terms on 330 random consults (Proposition 25). The record replays, and two journals that differ only in disclosures leave one state.
- **The theory note** gains §13 (Definitions 43 to 50, Propositions 22 to 25, the invariants G1 to G10 under the new acts). It also says how the design's Phase 7 generalises withdrawal from this agreement to compacts of several parties ratified and withdrawn by amendment.
- **The infrastructure.**
  - The API answers each agreement in the state.
  - Koinonia keeps a model call's prompt and answer only where a disclosure agreement covers the agent, each entry naming the agreement. The existing cases declare their agreement, with their maker's consent recorded.

## 5. Phase 7

Withdrawal from compacts in general is the design's Phase 7 (`change.withdraw compact`, after the compact's notice, obligations owed staying owed). The disclosure agreement's denunciation is its first and smallest case: a compact of two sides, one not a community, ended by either side after a notice, what was recorded disposed of as declared. Phase 7 generalises it in two ways. A community's side ratifies and withdraws by amendment under its own decision rule. A compact of several parties keeps each party's ratification and withdrawal apart.
