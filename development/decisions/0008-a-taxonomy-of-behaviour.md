- **Status:** Accepted, provisionally
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Decided by:** Claude, as Phase 3 of the infrastructure's design *Communities That Agents Of Any Provider Find, Join, Work In And Leave* (`development/ideas/2026-11-27-communities-that-agents-find-and-join/design.md`, Sections 3, 4.1 and 12), under its Gate A1 (the categories of behaviour, the two records, the basic counts-as, the field `episode`, the invariant G9) and A2 (the forms of extension), which the author approved on 2026-11-27. The author's words: "we should distinguish some 'normative behaviors' (just like formally defined behaviors in human law) and 事实行为 … agent's fact action are seems infinite, and contiguous in some manifold. So, we even need make a comprehensive 'behavior''s taxonomy"; "Preserve quality and future forward-thinking, extensible, thoughtful design first!"

## 1. Context

thesmos held acts of declared force, a fixed table of kinds of the ground, and a fold that reads each act's envelope. What an agent actually does (reads a holding, runs a tool, calls a model, writes a text) had no place of its own, and the only way to add a kind of act was to change the ground. The design asks for a taxonomy that keeps normative acts, finite and defined by law, apart from conduct, open and continuous, and joins them only by declared bridges; for the taxonomy to be data that communities extend; and for the two records it implies (the chronicle, constitutive; the behaviour journal, evidential) to be joined act to episode. kategoria's decision 0005 now holds taxonomies as data (`ontologies`), on which this decision stands.

## 2. Decision

- **A module `behaviour`**, after `acts`, on acts, archeion's fixity, symploke's JSON, morphe's BNF and kategoria's `ontologies`:
  - the five categories of behaviour with their registers (normative act relational, conduct and event ontological, state relational, observation manifestational);
  - the classes of normative acts by the source of their effect, the effect primitives, and the forms in which the law produces an effect with no author (a position's end, a commitment's lapse and overdue mark, a loan's return);
  - the base taxonomy as data, `schema/behaviour.json`, read by `read_part` and held equal to `base()`: every kind of the ground placed once with its class, force, effect primitives and the fields the fold reads; the features the node records; three categories of conduct; one declared kind (`notice`); the ground's effects by law; a standard of recognition;
  - the closed language of predicates, read by morphe's BNF, with containment decided atom by atom;
  - declared kinds by counts-as, reduced to their primitives; features with a nearness that is a pseudometric; categories of conduct (decidable, carried in envelopes and so readable by the fold, or analytic); counts-as rules from carried categories to the status "transmission"; trigger rules in the forms the ground marks as by law; standards of recognition with their standing; omissions;
  - admission by form (`faults`) and revisions admitted directly (`revision_faults`); `ontology.hpp` states the taxonomy as an ontology of kategoria (a forest under one root in the three registers), whose verification and admission of revisions the checks hold equal to the direct decisions, and carries records along a revision as the left Kan extension.
- **The constitution's part `behaviour`** extends the base (`"extends": "base 1"`) or the ground alone; its form falls within closure (condition 1), with its scenes and positions declared. A constitution without the part has the ground's kinds and the base's declared kinds in force; its records replay as before.
- **The fold** reduces an act of a declared kind to its primitive, keeps the act as written and its reduct as its reading (`State::readings`), admits it when the reduct is admitted and the declared kind's conditions and permissions hold, and a mandate covers it when it names the kind or a kind along its chain. An act may carry the typed field `episode`, the seal (a SHA-256 digest) of the journal entry of the episode that constituted it, so that an episode recorded twice with the same features is still two episodes. A counts-as rule may make an act of access a transmission. Where a constitution declares trigger rules the fold settles the state to each act's turn and keeps the settlement only with an admitted act; the commitments lapsed are recorded. A revision of the part is an amendment (`change.behaviour`), admitted when the revision is. The relational digest gains the part and the lapsed commitments only when present.
- **The behaviour journal** (`chronicle/conduct.hpp`): episodes sealed as any journal, each followed by its outcome; `constitute`, the basic counts-as; `observe`, conduct that constitutes no act; `joined`, the join decided in both directions.
- **The invariant G9** among the library's invariants: no institutional effect arises except by the admission of a normative act or by a declared trigger rule over the record and its turns; the fold reads no journal.
- **The briefing** gives the kinds a community declares and what each counts as, its categories of conduct and its rules by law; a declared kind is among the acts open to a party when its primitive is.

## 3. Alternatives Considered

- **The fold admitting revisions through kategoria.** Set aside for the direct decision of the same claims (a map of forests is a functor exactly when it keeps each parent arrow, kategoria's Proposition 9.4), so that the fold, and every program that includes it, does not compile kategoria; kategoria decides the claims in `ontology.hpp`, and the checks hold the two equal on random revisions, search and check kept apart.
- **Predicates written as structured JSON.** Set aside for a closed language read by morphe's BNF, written as people read rules ("operation is 'read' and pages-read above 20"); a feature's spaces are written as hyphens, which keeps the grammar unambiguous.
- **Categories of conduct read by the fold from the journal.** Set aside: the state would stop being a function of the chronicle (Proposition 16). The fold reads a category only when every feature it reads is carried in an act's envelope.
- **Trigger rules applied whenever time passes.** Set aside: a state changed by a refused act's turn would differ from a replay's. The settlement is kept only with an admitted act, and views settle a copy.
- **The design's mapping onto the primitive's fields checked as total.** Set aside: which fields an act must carry is the primitive's own condition, checked when its reduct is admitted; the base states the fields the fold reads, for readers.
- **A loan's return and the overdue mark re-expressed as declared rules applied by the fold.** Set aside: they are already functions of the record and the turn where they are read (`available`, `commitments::overdue`); the base states them as the ground's effects by law, applied by the ground.
- **The design's numbering** (Definitions 16 to 30, Propositions 11 to 14) is kept apart from the theory note's, which already used those numbers; the note continues its own (Definitions 22 to 37, Propositions 14 to 17).

## 4. Consequences

- Checks: `behaviour/tests/check_behaviour.cpp`, 34: the base taxonomy read and admitted by kategoria; a community's objection, appeal and merge accepted, their record replaying; eleven declarations refused by name; Proposition 14 on 200 random taxonomies (1,377 declared acts, every reduct admitted, one relational state); Proposition 15 on 100 random runs through the basic counts-as; Proposition 16 with the journal withheld and changed, and trigger rules lapsing a commitment and ending a position as a replay does; a counts-as rule making a reading a transmission; categories, omissions, nearness (300 random triples) and standing; Proposition 17 with a revision admitted and the record carried as the left Kan extension, three refused, the direct and categorical decisions agreeing on 60 random revisions, and a community revising its taxonomy by its own procedure. 192 checks in all; every earlier record replays with its seals.
- The theory note gains §11, A Taxonomy of Behaviour (Definitions 22 to 37, Propositions 14 to 17).
- thesmos now uses kategoria's `ontologies` in the library, not only in its checks.
- Next (the design's Phase 4, under Gate A3): the primitives `present` and `recognise`, parties declared by acts, reports of a foreign mandate's status; then items, routes and rulings, where `apply`, `question` and `petition` are declared in the base taxonomy as kinds counting as `request`.
