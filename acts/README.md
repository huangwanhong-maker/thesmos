# acts: Acts of Declared Force

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

Five forces after Searle; the ground's kinds of act and the force of each; an act's typed envelope and its free body. The envelope's canonical text prefixes every value by its length, so that two envelopes share a text exactly when equal.
