# acts: Acts of Declared Force

Status: `draft` (decision 0003).

## 1. Concept

Five forces after Searle; the ground's kinds of act and the force of each; an act's typed envelope and its free body. The envelope's canonical text prefixes every value by its length, so that two envelopes share a text exactly when equal. The parts of a mandate that passports read (decision 0007) are written into the text only when set, so every envelope written before them is written again exactly. Since decision 0009 the ground holds `present` (assertive: an agent presenting its passport at a host) and `recognise` (declarative: a community recognising another at a level); since decision 0010 `agree` (commissive: a side's ratification of a disclosure agreement) and `denounce` (declarative: its denunciation). Since decision 0011 a `request` that names a type opens an item of that type, and the base taxonomy declares `apply`, `question` and `petition` as kinds counting as such requests.
